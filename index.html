How Geoline works

01

Name a U.S. place

Start with any city or town in the United States. Geoline finds it and drops a marker on the map.

02

Take turns

Each player names a new place. Every new location connects to the previous one, building a shared route.

03

Never cross the route

If your new line crosses any earlier non-adjacent line, you lose the round immediately.

04

No repeats

A place already used in the current game cannot be played again. Think ahead and trap your opponent.

Example

Seattle → Denver → Miami → Boston...

The map gets harder every turn. A city that looks safe can create a long line straight across an earlier segment.
